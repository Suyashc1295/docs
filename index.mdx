HireOtto is a remote Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that gives your AI assistant direct access to Google Ads. Instead of switching between tools, you can ask Claude, Cursor, or any MCP-enabled AI to create campaigns, adjust bids, pull performance reports, and manage keywords — all in plain English.

## How it works

HireOtto runs as a remote MCP server — there's nothing to install locally. You configure your AI tool to connect to the HireOtto endpoint, authorize access to your Google Ads account, and start managing ads through conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your AI tool">
    Add the HireOtto MCP server URL to your AI assistant's MCP configuration. Works with Claude (Web, Desktop& Code), ChatGPT, Make.com, and any remote MCP-compatible client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Google Ads">
    Complete the OAuth flow to grant HireOtto read/write access to your Google Ads account. Your credentials are never stored on HireOtto servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start managing ads">
    Ask your AI assistant to list campaigns, create ad groups, pull performance metrics, or adjust budgets — using plain English.
  </Step>
</Steps>

HireOtto also supports Google Search Console reporting in beta, so you can analyze organic queries, page performance, country/device breakdowns, CSV exports, and URL indexing from the same AI workflow.
